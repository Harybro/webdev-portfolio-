Historic town reconstruction

Project Overview

A 1:1 3D reconstruction of a Scottish town as it would of looked in the 1850s with as much historic detail as possible. Recrating the town and tis buildings using real world dimensions to maintain accurate scale and proportions.

Key Features:

  • 1:1 scale Reconstructoin
  • Real-World terrain imported geographical elevation data to recreate the natural landscape and surrounding terrain in unity
  • Historically Accurate Buildings based on historical maps, photographs, mesurements and written records with an emphasis on avoiding unsupported assumptions
  • Historical research on individual buildings to establish their historical appearance, purpose, ownership and changes.
  • Independent Development ongoing, large-scale person project involving 3D enviroment developement, geospatial data processing, research and technical problem-solving
  • Scale is over a 5kmx5km area including the towns sournding villages and farmsteads

Technologies Used:

Unity, QGIS, C#, Digital Elevation Models, Unity Terrain System, 3D Enviorment Development

Project Details:

Duration: 1+ year | Status: work in progess | Team Size: solo

This is a personal project that I've been working on for over a year. It has helped me develop my independent problem-solving skills, learn new technologies, and improve my research abilities through the careful examination of historical sources to ensure the reconstruction is as accurate as possible. Working on this long-term project has also strengthened my ability to manage my own work, overcome challenges, and stay committed to a project over an extended period.
